Capital Structure and Dividend Policy of Wipro
*Project Overview
This minor project report evaluates the corporate financial management framework of Wipro Limited , analyzing its capital configurations, dividend payout sustainability, and operational risk metrics across a six-year horizon (FY 2019–2024).

* This project evaluates Wipro’s corporate finance architecture (2019–2024) by analyzing the relationship between its debt-equity mix, dividend payouts, and risk exposure. The findings show that Wipro maintains a conservative, equity-heavy capital structure alongside an earnings-reinvestment strategy that keeps dividend payouts under 6%. Despite minor debt fluctuations and operational leverage volatility in 2022, the company successfully rebounded by 2024, demonstrating strong financial stability and expanding investor confidence. Ultimately, the study highlights how Wipro effectively balances corporate liquidity with strategic choices to ensure sustainable, long-term growth.
* This project utilizes a quantitative financial ratio analysis and single-firm case study approach to evaluate the corporate finance architecture of Wipro Limited over a six-year period (2019–2024). Rather than relying on a historical literature review or qualitative data triangulation, the methodology is strictly driven by quantitative secondary data triangulation. Raw accounting and market metrics were systematically gathered and cross-verified from high-authority sources, including Wipro’s official corporate annual reports, sustainability disclosures, and stock market databases like Moneycontrol. These figures were then modeled into structured ratio blocks to analyze solvency, liquidity management, shareholder distribution sustainability, and operational risk sensitivities. Finally, a chronological trend analysis was applied to these metrics to pinpoint key structural shifts—such as the financial volatility and debt restructuring seen in 2022—allowing for data-backed strategic recommendations regarding Wipro's long-term capital optimization.
* Finding 1: Conservative Leverage Structure. Wipro maintained a highly stable, equity-heavy capital architecture, financing approximately 65% of its total assets through equity by 2024.
* Finding 2: Strategic Reinvestment Policy. Aside from a major payout spike in 2022 (26.89%), the company strictly prioritized future growth by keeping its dividend payout ratio below 6% for most years.
* Finding 3: Resilient Debt Recovery. Despite experiencing high operational risk and cash flow tightening in 2020 and 2022, Wipro successfully rebounded by 2023, expanding its Debt Service Coverage Ratio to a healthy 2.42.
* Finding 4: Rising Market Optimism. Driven by robust operational adjustments, investor confidence expanded significantly, lifting the company's P/E ratio from 17.66 to 22.71 between 2023 and 2024
